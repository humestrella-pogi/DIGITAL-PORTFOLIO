# Activity 3 — Social Media Infographics and Mini Project Documentation

## 📌 Overview

Activity 3 focused on creating social media infographics and documenting
the creative process behind the mini project.

The activity combined graphic design, information organization,
visual storytelling, and digital communication.

## 💡 Concept

The concept was developed around presenting information in a format
that is easy to understand and suitable for social media.

Because users often encounter information quickly while scrolling,
the infographic needed to communicate its main message through:

- Short text
- Visual elements
- Clear hierarchy
- Appropriate spacing
- Consistent colors
- Engaging composition

## 🎨 Design Choices

### Visual Hierarchy

The most important information was given greater visual emphasis
through size, placement, and contrast.

### Color

The established color palette was maintained to create consistency
between the different outputs.

### Typography

Readable typefaces and different font weights were used to distinguish
headings, supporting information, and details.

### Layout

Information was divided into sections to prevent the infographic
from becoming visually overwhelming.

### Visual Elements

Icons, shapes, images, and other graphical elements were used to
support the information rather than distract from it.

## 🛠️ Creative Process

1. Identified the main topic and purpose.
2. Determined the target audience.
3. Gathered and organized the information.
4. Selected the most important points.
5. Created an initial layout.
6. Applied the color palette and typography.
7. Added supporting graphics.
8. Adjusted hierarchy and spacing.
9. Reviewed the design for readability.
10. Finalized the infographic and documented the process.

## 📱 Final Output

The final infographics were designed specifically for digital and
social media viewing.

The design prioritizes quick comprehension while maintaining a
consistent and visually engaging presentation.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

Through this activity, I learned how to transform information into
visual content that can communicate a message effectively.

The activity also strengthened my understanding of visual hierarchy,
branding consistency, typography, color selection, and digital
communication.
