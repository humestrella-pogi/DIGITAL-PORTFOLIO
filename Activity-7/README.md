
# ACTIVITY 7: COMPARATIVE RESUME GENERATION USING MULTIPLE LLMs

**Course:** GE 4120 – 21st Century IT Skills  
**Program:** Bachelor of Science in Nursing  
**Section:** BSN 4F  
**Activity Type:** Individual AI-Assisted Resume Generation and Comparative Analysis

---

## 📌 Activity Overview

This activity focuses on exploring and comparing the capabilities of different Large Language Models (LLMs), specifically ChatGPT and Claude AI, in generating a professional one-page resume. Using the same personal information, educational background, clinical experiences, leadership positions, and achievements, both AI platforms were instructed to create a professional resume suitable for future employment opportunities.

The activity evaluates the generated resumes based on content organization, professional summary, language and tone, resume relevance, formatting and readability, additional recommendations, and overall quality. Through this comparison, the activity aims to demonstrate how AI can assist in professional document preparation while emphasizing the importance of accuracy, authenticity, and critical evaluation.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the capabilities of different Large Language Models in professional document generation.
- Explore how AI can assist in creating and organizing professional resumes.
- Compare the outputs of ChatGPT and Claude AI using common evaluation criteria.
- Identify the strengths and limitations of AI-generated professional documents.
- Develop skills in evaluating and refining AI-generated content.
- Recognize the importance of accuracy and authenticity when presenting personal qualifications and experiences.

## 📚 Topics Covered

1. Introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs)
2. Introduction to AI-Assisted Resume Generation
3. Creating a Professional Resume Using ChatGPT
4. Generating a Professional Resume Using Claude AI
5. Comparing AI-Generated Resume Outputs
6. Evaluation of Content Organization and Professional Summary
7. Assessment of Language, Tone, Formatting, and Readability
8. Identification of Strengths, Limitations, and Recommendations
9. Overall Comparative Analysis of ChatGPT and Claude AI

## 💡 Key Learnings and Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that different Large Language Models can generate varying outputs even when provided with the same instructions and personal information. I gained a better understanding of how AI can improve professional writing, organize qualifications, highlight relevant experiences, and present personal achievements in a more appropriate format. Comparing ChatGPT and Claude AI also helped me recognize that each platform has its own approach to structuring and presenting information.

One challenge I encountered was identifying which generated resume was more accurate, relevant, and suitable for professional applications, especially since the two platforms differed in their organization, wording, and recommendations. I addressed this by developing a comparative table and evaluating both outputs based on specific criteria, while also checking whether the information accurately represented my actual qualifications and experiences. I realized that an AI-generated resume should not automatically be considered complete without proper review and personalization.

As a nursing student preparing for future professional opportunities, I can apply this knowledge when creating resumes, cover letters, and other employment-related documents. I can further improve my use of AI by providing more specific instructions, prioritizing relevant clinical experiences, and ensuring that all qualifications and achievements are accurately presented. Most importantly, I learned that human judgment, critical thinking, authenticity, and personal accountability remain essential when using AI-generated content.
