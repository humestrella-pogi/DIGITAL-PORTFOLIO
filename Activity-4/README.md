
# ACTIVITY 4: GOOGLE DRIVE PRESENTATION

**Program:** Bachelor of Science in Nursing  
**Section:** BSN 4F  
**Activity Type:** Group Presentation

## 📌 Activity Overview

This activity focuses on Google Drive, a cloud-based storage service that allows users to store, organize, access, and share files online. The presentation introduces its essential features, functions, and practical applications in digital file management and collaboration.

The activity was conducted to develop an understanding of how cloud-based technology improves accessibility, productivity, and information sharing.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and importance of Google Drive.
- Understand how Google Drive works.
- Identify its main features and functions.
- Learn how to organize, upload, search, and share files.
- Understand the importance of sharing permissions and access control.

## 📚 Topics Covered

1. Introduction to Google Drive
2. How Google Drive Works
3. Purpose of Google Drive
4. Main Features of Google Drive
5. Functions of Google Drive
6. Interactive 15-Question Quiz

## 💡 Key Learnings and Reflection

Through this activity, I learned how Google Drive makes file storage, organization, and collaboration more accessible and efficient. I became more familiar with its functions, particularly uploading files, creating folders, renaming documents, searching for files, and managing sharing permissions.

Preparing the presentation also helped strengthen my communication, teamwork, and information-organizing skills. I realized that cloud storage is useful not only for academic activities but also for managing digital documents and collaborating with others.

As a nursing student, I can apply these skills when organizing research materials, storing academic requirements, preparing group presentations, and managing learning resources. However, confidential patient information must always be protected through appropriate privacy and access controls.

## 📂 Activity Output

**Presentation:** Google Drive Presentation

The presentation includes an introduction to Google Drive, its purpose, features, functions, and a 15-question knowledge assessment.

## 🚀 Future Application and Improvement

In future activities, I can use Google Drive to improve file organization, collaborate with classmates, and manage academic documents. The presentation could also be improved by including a live demonstration of uploading files, creating folders, sharing documents, and managing access permissions.
