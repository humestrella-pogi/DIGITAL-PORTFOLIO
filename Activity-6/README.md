
# ACTIVITY 6: MAKING A WEBSITE

## 📌 Activity Overview

This activity focuses on developing an advocacy website entitled **"Be the Reason: Choose Kindness, Stop Bullying."** The project aims to raise awareness about bullying and encourage individuals to contribute to creating a safer, more compassionate, and inclusive school community.

The website presents educational information about different forms of bullying, including physical, verbal, social, and cyberbullying, as well as their potential effects on an individual's emotional well-being, self-confidence, relationships, and sense of belonging.

Through the use of Replit and Artificial Intelligence (AI), the activity demonstrates how digital technology can be utilized to develop an interactive educational platform that promotes empathy, responsible behavior, and positive social change.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the basic concepts and processes involved in website development.
- Explore the use of Replit and AI tools in creating a digital platform.
- Develop an accessible, informative, and user-friendly website.
- Organize educational content effectively for the intended audience.
- Promote awareness about bullying and its different forms.
- Utilize digital technology to encourage social responsibility and positive behavioral change.

## 📚 Topics Covered

1. Introduction to Website Development
2. Understanding the Purpose of an Advocacy Website
3. Introduction to the "Be the Reason: Choose Kindness, Stop Bullying" Project
4. Identifying Different Forms of Bullying
5. Understanding the Effects of Bullying
6. Strategies for Recognizing and Responding to Bullying
7. Supporting Affected Individuals and Reporting Incidents
8. Website Development Using Replit and AI
9. Interactive Website Features and Kindness Pledge

## 💡 Key Learnings and Reflection

Through this activity, I learned how website development can be used as a platform for communicating important social issues and encouraging meaningful community participation. I gained a better understanding of how to write effective prompts, organize website content, design a user-friendly interface, and utilize Replit and AI tools in developing a digital platform.

One challenge I encountered was translating my advocacy concept into an organized and engaging website that could effectively communicate its message to different audiences. I addressed this by refining my instructions, organizing the educational content, and checking whether the website's features worked as intended. This experience taught me that developing a functional website requires creativity, careful planning, and attention to the needs of its intended users.

As a nursing student, I can apply these skills in developing educational websites and digital platforms related to health promotion, disease prevention, and community health awareness. I can further improve my website development skills by exploring more interactive features, improving accessibility, and ensuring that the information presented is accurate and understandable.
