
# ACTIVITY 5: AI TOOLS ACTIVITY

**Course:** GE 4120 – 21st Century IT Skills  
**Program:** Bachelor of Science in Nursing  
**Section:** BSN 4F  
**Activity Type:** Individual AI-Assisted Learning Activity

---

## 📌 Activity Overview

This activity focuses on exploring the capabilities of Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly ChatGPT, as a tool for improving study habits and academic preparation. It involves creating and refining prompts to generate study plans, comparing AI-generated outputs, and evaluating how prompt structure and specificity influence the quality and relevance of responses.

The activity aims to develop an understanding of effective prompt engineering and demonstrate how AI can support students in organizing their learning activities, managing study time, and understanding complex academic subjects, particularly nursing topics such as nephritis.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the capabilities of ChatGPT as an AI-powered learning tool.
- Develop effective prompts to generate relevant and organized study materials.
- Compare general and specialized prompts based on their generated outputs.
- Identify the importance of clarity and specificity in prompt engineering.
- Explore how AI can improve study planning, knowledge retention, and academic preparation.

## 📚 Topics Covered

1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and ChatGPT
2. Understanding Prompt Engineering
3. Creating an Original Study Plan Prompt
4. Refining Prompts for More Specific Learning Outcomes
5. Generating a Specialized Study Plan for Nephritis
6. Comparing Original and Refined Prompt Outputs
7. Evaluating the Effectiveness of AI-Assisted Learning

## 💡 Key Learnings and Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that the effectiveness of AI-generated responses largely depends on the clarity, structure, and specificity of the prompts provided. I discovered that general prompts can produce flexible study plans, while detailed prompts generate more focused and subject-specific learning activities. This helped me understand the importance of providing clear instructions, learning objectives, and appropriate study strategies when interacting with AI tools.

One challenge I encountered was determining how to make a prompt sufficiently detailed to produce comprehensive and relevant study materials. I addressed this by refining my original prompt and incorporating specific instructions, topics, and learning strategies. Comparing the two outputs also helped me recognize the strengths and limitations of different prompting approaches.

As a nursing student, I can apply these skills when preparing for examinations, reviewing complicated nursing concepts, and developing a deeper understanding of diseases and their pathophysiology. I can further improve my use of AI by incorporating active recall, practice examinations, clinical case scenarios, and rationales into my prompts. However, AI-generated information should always be evaluated and verified using reliable references.

## 📂 Activity Output

**AI Tool Used:** ChatGPT

The activity includes an original prompt, a refined prompt, their corresponding AI-generated outputs, and a comparative analysis discussing their differences, strengths, limitations, and effectiveness in supporting academic learning.

## 🚀 Future Application and Improvement

In future academic activities, I can use prompt engineering to create more personalized and structured study plans, nursing reviewers, practice examinations, and clinical case analyses. The activity could also be improved by incorporating measurable learning outcomes, evidence-based references, laboratory interpretation, pharmacological management, and nursing interventions into the generated study materials.
